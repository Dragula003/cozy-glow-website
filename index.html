Our Story

Born from a love of warmth & fragrance

The Cozy Glow began in a small kitchen with a passion for creating spaces that feel safe, warm, and beautiful. Every candle and wax sachet is handcrafted in small batches, ensuring the utmost care in each piece that leaves our hands.

We believe your home deserves the best — pure soy wax, phthalate-free fragrances, and botanicals that tell a story. No fillers, no shortcuts.
